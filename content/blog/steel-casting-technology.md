### **Common Casting Defects and Their Root Causes**

Knowing about casting defects is important for quality. We've found a few big problems:

* Porosity: Caused by trapped gases during solidification
* Shrinkage defects: Result from uneven cooling rates
* Cold shut: Occurs when molten metal fails to fuse completely
* Inclusions: Foreign materials trapped within the casting

### **Advanced Inspection Technologies**

Today, we use new ways to check for defects. Our non-destructive tests give us detailed info without harming the casting:

* Ultrasonic testing for internal structure evaluation
* X-ray and radiographic inspection
* Magnetic particle and liquid penetrant examinations
* Automated visual inspection systems

> "Precision in inspection is the guardian of manufacturing quality." - Manufacturing Engineering Insights
